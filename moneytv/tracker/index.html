How it works

Draft. Every Monday, put $10K of stock into your lineup. The draft closes 30 minutes after the market opens.

Compete. The top balance wins the week. Your lineup carries over to the next one.

Climb. A weekly board, plus an all-time rank by average weekly gain, so newer players compete fairly.

The MoneyTV Tracker is a fantasy league for education and entertainment. It uses no real money and is not investment advice.
