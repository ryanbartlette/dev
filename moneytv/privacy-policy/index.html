This Privacy Policy describes how M3L1 LLC, doing business as MoneyTV ("MoneyTV," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, listen to our podcast, or otherwise interact with us.

01Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you subscribe to our newsletter, submit a guest booking request, contact us by email or phone, or otherwise communicate with MoneyTV. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and any message content you send us.

We also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit our website, including your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and referring URLs, through cookies and similar tracking technologies.

02How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve our website and podcast, respond to inquiries and booking requests, send newsletters and marketing communications you've opted into (such as Buy the Dip), process sponsorship and advertising inquiries, and analyze site usage to improve the user experience.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

03Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how visitors use our site, and support analytics and advertising features. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

04Third-Party Services

We may use third-party service providers to help operate our website, distribute our podcast, manage email and SMS communications, and process analytics (for example, podcast hosting platforms, email marketing tools, and website analytics providers). These providers may have access to your information only as needed to perform their services and are obligated not to use it for other purposes.

05Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your information with service providers who assist us in operating our business, when required by law or legal process, to protect the rights, property, or safety of MoneyTV or others, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

06Your Rights & Choices

You may unsubscribe from our email newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe link included in every email. You may also contact us directly to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact us to make a request.

07Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08Children's Privacy

MoneyTV is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

09Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Your continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of those changes.

10Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at hello@moneytvpod.com or 615.266.4111.
