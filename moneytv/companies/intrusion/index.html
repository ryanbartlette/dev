About Intrusion Inc.

Intrusion is a Plano, Texas cybersecurity company focused on prevention-first network security and advanced threat intelligence.

Its Shield platform evaluates inbound and outbound network connections against the company’s proprietary Global Threat Engine, allowing known malicious connections to be blocked at the connection layer.

The Global Threat Engine has been building IP and DNS reputation intelligence since 2001 and now covers approximately 8.5 billion combinations. Intrusion applies that same intelligence across on-premise infrastructure, cloud environments and endpoints.
